# Fashion Police Squad

_August 29, 2026_

<p>
	<img class="img-logo snes-container no-padding" src="/images/fps-logo.png">
</p>

_It's not what you wear...It's how you wear it!_

### First Impression

A couple of years ago, a game came up in the recommended for me during the Steam Next Fest. A Doom clone where you are the literal fashion police, using an arsenal of weapons to "solve" different fashion crimes - by...well, shooting them of course. Sounded like a good time. I downloaded it, and after an initial fun play, it sat in my wishlist for a while until one day I finally decided to bite the bullet and finally give it a real play through. While Fashion Police Squad doesn't bring anything really new to the table - essentially its just a Doom mod - what it does do, it does it with style, fitting of course the games style first approach.

### Story

In terms of story its short and sweet with no real complaints. There is a Mr Evil Bad Guy™ who is trying to make everyone unfashionable because he grew up with hand-me-downs and is now going to be the most fashionable person in the world. You spend the story uncovering who it is, confronting, and eventually dismantling the plan in form of the BFG (Big Fashionable Gun). The story happens in between levels, and in the form of little cutscenes between them, typically while meeting up with your liaison Deep Coat, who gives you information on the baddie, who ended up aptly named Runt Coat, Deep Coats little brother. Runt Coat turns out to lure you into a trap, reveling he is indeed Turn Coat (yes I'm serious)!

While nothing crazy or a story that will really stick with me, the story was mostly just a means to an end. Nothing bad, just a silly little plot mover to justify bouncing around through the levels. Not really average, because the writing was funny enough to justify actually reading it. Sometimes simple is good, and if it ain't broke, don't fix it.

### Look & Feel

If you can imagine a brightly colored doom set in a metropolis, filled to the brim with fashion culture (and the before mentioned fashion crimes), you'd pretty much hit the nail on the head for the look of FPS. In terms of feel, again it's a doom clone. You kinda know exactly what you're getting. Outside from the controls being pretty run of the mill, in terms of how doom clones actually don't seek to reinvent the wheel, hands down the most interesting thing going for FPS is its rock paper scissors combat. You have to correctly identify the fashion crime, and use the appropriate weapon to fix it, this leads to a careful dance, especially in the more crowded encounters. 

I really loved how the game forces you to play by its rules, and having you do these micro puzzles for each encounter. Take for example this encounter; There is a room with balconies, where multiple bomb throwing guys in loose suits will bomb you from above, scooter guys wearing bright neon shirts, and grey suit normal mobs all in one go. Biggest threat would be the bomb guys, so I have to dodge all the rest of the ground mobs, use the sewing gun to dispatch. Next I have to switch to the color shotgun to add a little color to the grey suit mobs, and finally suck out the brightly colored from the scooter bros with the guns alt fire. Having to prioritize enemies with different weapons, as opposed to other Doom like games, where you can just blast away brings a nice new feel to the genre, and I hope more games take to doing something similar. You don't have to reinvent the wheel, but a little unique perspective is always a welcome touch.

### Sound & Music

The way the fashion and gay communities around the world rally around electronic music needs to be studied. The influence is clear in FPS, and it makes you feel like you are in a vogue ballroom, which I believe is the intended effect. I don't really have any stick out tracks, as they kinda blend to me, but the little bits at the end of a level where you have the Parole Parade was my favorite. 

### Fun Factor

I had a lot of fun with this one. The gameplay is solid, and never really frustrating through the entirety of the game. A lot of encounters, and a few really fun boss fights to boot. They really pulled out all the stops of the SuperHaxor1337Guy (or whatever his name was lmao), and was probably the biggest highlight for me. It was hard to not enjoy FPS, and was basically a big smile from start to finish. The best parts for me was following along the story, it was nice a silly, and gave the world character. The fashion crimes were cute, and a nice touch that will likely age funny as fashion is every changing. I wonder how this game will look in terms of trends in 20 years?

### Overall

Fashion Police Squad fits right into my short and sweet category. Never overstays its welcome, and I greatly appreciated being able to finish this over the course of a few days. I loved the rock paper scissors elements to the combat, and it was a silly story that had me laughing and smiling all the way through. I think for those of us who love boomer shooters, this was a neat new take on the genre, while still respecting its roots, and putting combat first over everything. I think this is a definite buy, and I hope to play more unique FPS's like this one in the future.
