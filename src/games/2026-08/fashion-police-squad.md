### First Impression

A couple of years ago, a game came up in the reccomended for me during the Steam Next Fest. A Doom clone where you are the literal fashion police, using an arsenal of weapons to "solve" different fashion crimes - by...well, shooting them of course. Sounded like a good time. I downloaded it, and after an initial fun play, it sat in my wishlist for a while until one day I finally decidewd to bite the bullet and finally give it a real playthrough. While Fashion Police Squad doesnt bring anything really new to the table - essentially its just a Doom mod - what it does do, it does it with style, fitting of course the games style first approach.

### Story

In terms of story its short and sweet with no real complaints. There is a Mr Evil Bad Guy™ who is trying to make everyone unfashionable because he grew up with hand-me-downs and is now going to be the most fashionable person in the world. You spend the story uncovering who it is, confronting, and eventually dismantelling the plan in form of the BFG (Big Fashionable Gun). The story happens in between levels, and in the form of little cutscenes between them, typically while meeting up with your liason Deep Coat, who gives you information on the baddie, who ended up aptly named Runt Coat, Deep Coats little brother. Runt Coat turns out to lure you into a trap, revieling he is indeed Turn Coat (yes I'm serious)!

While nothing crazy or a story that will really stick with me, the story was mostly just a means to an end. Nothing bad, just a silly little plot mover to justify bouncing around through the levels. Not really average, because the writing was funny enough to justify actually reading it. Sometimes simple is good, and if it aint broke, dont fix it.
