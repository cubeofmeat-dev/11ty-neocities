# Wolfenstein: The Old Blood

<p>
    <img class="img-logo snes-container no-padding" src="/images/wob-logo.png">
</p>

_January 30, 2026_

When you play a good game, often times you just want more. And when waiting for the sequel, a little more is all you need to satisfy that itch. In comes The Old Blood - a standalone expansion to The New Order, that sets up the prequel story leading into the first (MachineGames) game. Simply put, its more Wolfenstein: The New Order, just set a few years before the start of that game, with the same mechanics, feel, and fun of its predecessor. 

What I really liked about this expansion, is that it was more of what I already liked about the previous game. Still set in the same universe, and still taking place as BJ. This entry kinda rectifies some of the older games fumbling around with the paranormal, and has you trying to infiltrate Castle Wolfenstein to get ahold of coordinates to DeathHeads secret nazi base. Naturally things go wrong and you almost immediately get busted trying to pose as a nazi officer, and you and your compadre are taken to the gulag, and BJ is forced to sneak his way out. On first playthrough I remember this being kinda shitty as you dont get to go guns blazing but instead are sneaking your way around, to find your way to a more advantageous spot. Not bad on second playthrough, and Im certainly glad that it didnt overstay its welcome. Now onto the fun part.

Once you get outfitted with weapons again, and find yourself blasting your way out of the Castle, I found something very familiar - a cable car section! Just like Return to Castle Wolfenstein all the way back in 2003! "Is this game just a modern retelling of Return to Castle Wolfenstein?" he asked, while maiming about 100 nazis on the way to activate the cable car to escape. A welcome surprise, and I would not have had the context if I had not played the previous entries. After getting into the next sections, fight off more baddies, meet up with the resistance, and attempt to find more information about DeathsHead's compound, you find a plot to resurrect an ancient evil - the paranormal division is here in full swing.

<p>
    <img class="img-game snes-container no-padding" src="/images/wob-img1.jpg">
</p>

The next sections of the game are a zombie romp through an old german town as you desperately try to find the old chapel and catacombs so that you can put a stop to Mrs. Evil Bad Woman™, and its less about memorable story at this point and more about cool experiences. How about a section of sneaking through a bunch of guards and Uber Soldats? Sure, next. How about running through the same area again, with a mech this time with nothing standing in your way? Awesome! The game more or less takes beats like that until the end, where you face off with a giant monster man before killing him and heading out to DeathsHeads compound, where the first game is about to take place.

What I liked about this entry, is that it was simple, stupid, and didnt over stay its welcome. A perfect addition to keep the palate moist while waiting for the next game. Honestly a lot of good fun here, and coming in only 5 hours, a perfect weekend game. Not something I would play on repeat, but something I would definitely replay when Im feeling like shooting some nazis.
