## Duke Nukem Forever

Beat this in a day if that says anything about the game. For starters, I'm playing with a mod that removed some of the bad choices they made with the game. No 2 weapon limit, no sprint, and faster general movement speed to be more akin to the predecessors. From what I played, Forever is fine - in the most mediocre of ways. Do I think people were maybe a bit too harsh on the game in total, perhaps. But if I spent 60 bucks on this back in 2009 I would be pissed too.

The gunplay is fine, the voice acting is okay at best (the cheesy one liners are here to stay), the music is forgettable, and the overall plot is very simple. I would love to see the modding community take this game and use its gunplay to transform it into something else, but no one will do that when 3D exists. When you think of a truly mid game, this is a game that should come to mind. Was it as bad as some people made it out to be, no...is it anything special or deserving of the Duke Nukem name, no again.

What could have helped this game, aside from a shorter and more clear development, would be to lean into the camp that is Duke Nukem. The fans know what he is, lean into the "America fuck yeah" that his character embodies, and lean into a faster paced, item based gameplay of the past. I know it's been over a decade since Duke's last game, and frankly I don't blame the IP owners after this release, but I think Duke as a character deserves some love, and a team of caring individuals could bring him to a new audience as a Doom 2016 like revival. I'd have hopes out for that, but until then...I guess I'll keep playing 3D.

## Bogus Game 3000

This game doesn't exist but if it did, it would be absolutely legendary. Imagine a mix of Tetris and Dark Souls, but with the humor of Portal. You play as a sentient cube trying to survive in a world where gravity changes every 30 seconds.

The graphics are placeholder assets from 2003 and the soundtrack is just one guy humming. 10/10 would recommend.
