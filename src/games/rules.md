# What is my rating scale?

Letter grades representing the overall feel I have for a game. Largely weighing in fun factor, graphics, sound, experience, and replayability. The scoring I have is as followed:

F - Horrible game, turned off before even beating

D - Bad game, potentially would not play all the way through

C - Middle of the road, either frustrating or not worth replaying

B - Great game worth experiencing, good replayability, but not something you would play often

A - Masterpiece, worth replaying often, fun, otherwise perfect experience


# Examples:

F - No examples come to mind, I don't play that bad of games

D - ReCore - Not a terrible game, but didn't stick out enough to make me play it all the way through

C - Call of Duty United Offensive - Average experience, not really worth replaying

B - Rage - Good game worth experiencing, but far from masterpiece

A - Nuclear Throne - Nothing like it. Nearly perfect game with insane replayability
