# Metal Slug Tactics

_June 2026_

<p>
	<img class="img-logo snes-container no-padding" src="/images/mst-logo.png">
</p>

_"HEAVY MACHINE GUN!"_

### First Impression

This was totally one of those games that I saw during a Steam sale and I said out loud, "huh, Because I'm playing into the breach I'd love to play this.", And I was totally right. Another game in a similar vein, one of those games that's easy to pick up and put down. Except set in a world that I already care about, metal slug tactics offers a similar enough experience While maintaining something that is unique to metal slug, something that I wasn't expecting from this game at all. What I was expecting was a carbon copy of other tactic games, albeit I'm not really a tactic game snob, Because I don't really like them I feel like my opinion on what I like to get from them makes what I have to say Unique to my perspective. I loved everything about Metal Slug when I was a kid, and I've played just about every single one of them. Metal Slug Tactics was like a game made for me, at a time where I was playing some more tactical type games. What's really great about metal slug tactics is like into the breach, it is roguelike, and very easy to play small bursts. Something I appreciate more and more as I continue to grow older.

### Story

More of a story here than the game I played previously, metal slug tactics follows the Metal Slug crew in trying to stop Mr Evil Bad Guy™ By using guerrilla Warfare and hit and run tactics. Most of the story that you get Comes in the form of conversation between the characters, and less of a story that has like an overarching plot. Marco has quips with the commander and his team members, making the world feel a little bit more alive for a silly little tactics game.

### Look & Feel

It's a metal slug game. Amazing Sprite direction, Amazing animations, detailed backgrounds, and awesome music. I felt like my expectations were high for the SNK team to deliver a metal slug experience like they had in the past, and they really nailed it. What stuck out to me the most is how similar the tactic game plays to the actual run & gun game. Tactics rewards you for constantly being on the move, Giving you incentives to run around like a madman. The game rewards hasty gameplay, Which is great for me because I have zero patience. You roll with your crew in a tight pack, and playoff of each other getting combos and running through the map as if it was a traditional metal slug game. I really enjoyed that a lot about tactics, However it was a little bit more taxing on the brain, as the scope of some of the maps are quite large - and less easy to quickly wrap up a scenario.

### Sound & Music

Again, It's a metal slug game. I really don't have too much to say on the music outside of its great. From horns to guitars to drums, Metal Slug Tactics really encapsulates that old metal slug musical feel. This was a game where I would not turn off the sound, simply because it adds so much to the core experience.

### Fun Factor

There's a lot of fun to be had here, But I don't know if it's gonna be something that I would play for years and years. I think this is one of those games that you can get a solid 20 to 30 hours out of, and then have your fill. Not that there isn't a lot to be enjoyed here, but I think the simplicity of other tactic titles stick that over metal select tactics in that way. I would have liked to see metal slug tactics have a more dedicated story, because what they truly went for was an approachable roguelike title, and it serves that purpose well. Plenty of fun while you're playing it, but something I would pick up every now and again.

### Overall

I really enjoyed this game, and I'm not rating it low because it's not good at all. In fact I wanted to rate it something higher but I'm trying to give out less A's, And really only highlight the gains that I would continuously go back and play. Metal Select Tactics has a lot good going for it, just after that 20/30 hour mark I don't know if I would pick it up again until maybe a year later. Definitely worth on sale, or if you're a die hard tactic or Metal slug fan.
