# Escape From Tarkov

_July 2026_

<p>
	<img class="img-logo snes-container no-padding" src="/images/eft-logo.png">
</p>

_А ну, чики-брики и в дамки!_

### First Impression

Man. Where do I even begin with this one? Tarkov Is one of those games unlike anything else, Meaning that the sheer scope of this game eclipses virtually every other extraction shooter on the market. I have tried to get into Tarkov at least three other time, and was unsuccessful each time. Until one day, It just clicked. I have a hard time explaining why I feel like it suddenly clicked for me, Perhaps it was the people I'm playing with, perhaps it was a different outlook, perhaps it was getting a little more adjusted each time to the immense amount of systems at play here in this game. Largely, I feel like I have marathon to thank. I was broken on my gear fear, and now I'm able to play Tarkov without hoarding and experiencing the gameplay And the systems in its entirety, without fear of losing something worth a lot of Virtual cash in a virtual video game world. This is not a true fair first impression, because it's more like a 4th impression, But resetting my character and starting fresh Has really helped me to enjoy this run through much more than my previous ones.

### Story

For the first time in ever, tarkov now has a legit story! Since the game launched in 1.0, the story about terra group, Tarkov, PMCS, and the world evolving around the EMP attack, gives me Many more reasons To invest in this world. A lot of what stopped me from playing in previous attempts was I felt that I was just a minor character in all this mess. A minor character of a minor character if that . All that existed was side quests, and a lot of it felt empty. Go here, fetch that. It didn't make me feel like I was a character in the world. Now, although I am a minor character in this world , I feel my position here actually has weight. I make friends with traders, I do favors for them, I find items for the people in the world like water, food, guns, and even medicine. The story has you talking to a variety of people all with something Different to gain by being in Tarkov. Where I'm at now in the story is starting to uncover what the large organizations were doing in Tarkov , and I'm excited to get to learn more.

### Look & Feel

Now this is definitely going to rustle some feathers. I'm not a person who appreciates realism in games over a good art style. Tarkov absolutely prioritizes realism. Now that's fine, because that's exactly what it's going for - And when you look at clips of Tarkov, You know you're looking at Tarkov. However, I do think the look of Tarkov is one that is easily replicatable, and you see it in many other milsim extraction shooters. For me what Tarkov is lacking in look , it makes it up in gameplay, gunplay, Systems, And the sheer size of its world around you. Unlike other "realistic" games, Tarkov has the system of momentum. The weight mechanics in Tarkov place it above all of the copycats. You dynamically gain and lose speed depending on how much gear you are carrying. It affects your gun handling and your speed . Something a lot of games don't really take into consideration, in favor of making it feel more gamey. I think the fairest thing to say about Tarkov, Is this not a game , It's Tarkov. 

### Sound & Music

I think I can probably omit music here, because you only get a few selects on the main menu. And while they are bangers! I feel maybe some ambience could add a lot to the world. The majority of the music comes in forms of stings when you complete a mission, and some heavy metal when you're sorting your inventory. The sound on the other hand is a different beast. Sound is extremely important in a extraction game like this, in Tarkovs sound is very interesting. Not only do you have 3d audio, great dynamic range, and whatnot, Tarkov has dynamic audio in such a way where your sound is changed depending on what earphones you have on. Do you have a cheap pair? Poorer sound quality. High tier military tech, you could hear a mouse fart 500 meters away. Stuff like that really make the game interesting, and truly sets the tone for how deep this rabit hole goes.

### Fun Factor

Saying Tarkov is fun is like saying photoshop is fun in a way. An immense program that requires you to spend a lot of time learning the way IT does things. The experience is so rewarding when you dont just glue yourself to the wiki. Asking questions, and searching their solutions only to find the system was already built in and you can act on your questions in game is such a cool feeling. The other day I noticed an item in a game, a key ring, was a container. After a while of not doing that, I wondered "instead of taking up space in my alpha pack on keys, what if I put keys on this key ring, and consolidate 4 spaces down to 1?". It works, and thats how a lot of the community does it. Such a small thing, just leading to the most reward, because now I have 3 more inventory slots bitch. I think thats the feedback loop. The game is so packed with features that are unique to Tarkov, that once it clicks, it makes for an experience no other. Oh yeah and the gunplay is a lot of fun too.

### Overall

Playing this game with people can either make or break this game. A lot of the fun in Tarkov is the learning curve, and the randomness you face in every raid. To a degree, extraction games are just roguelike FPS games - jump into a raid, no idea what the loot is going to be like, no idea who is going to spawn, no idea what mission you are going to be able to complete. That loop keeps the game fresh, and makes coming back to it a couple times a week enjoyable. Would this be my main game? Absolutely not, but I really am having an enjoyable time playing this go round. Im excited to see where the game is going, BattleState is doing a lot more events, and making the game more chaotic and I really enjoy that. I will likely be keeping Tarkov as a background game for a while now, simply because its a PvE experience thats designed tough, and you dont have to worry about being griefed by people who dont have jobs and never stop playing. Part of the reason Marathon got so frustrating was that. I want to immerse myself in this world, and Tarkov does that right from boot. I remember an old joke from back in the day that sums it up perfect - In Soviet Russia, Tarkov plays you.

# Grade

B+
